02 / SIGNAL PROCESSING

Sampling & FFT checker.

Explore how a chosen sample rate and FFT length affect a signal measurement. Change the inputs to see the Nyquist margin and frequency resolution.

THE IDEA

Choose the measurement first.

For a highest signal frequency f, the sample rate must be greater than 2f to leave Nyquist margin. An N-point FFT has frequency-bin spacing fs / N and a capture duration N / fs.

  • Nyquist frequency: sample rate ÷ 2
  • FFT bin spacing: sample rate ÷ sample count
  • Capture duration: sample count ÷ sample rate

These are idealised calculations. Real measurements also depend on filtering, windowing, noise and the analogue front end.
