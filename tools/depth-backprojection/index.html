FROM IMAGE TO GEOMETRY

A pixel is a ray.

A depth image provides Z along the camera’s forward axis. The pinhole model then gives X = (u − cx)Z / fx and Y = (v − cy)Z / fy.

  • u and v are the pixel coordinates
  • focal lengths and principal point are in pixels
  • X points right, Y down and Z forward in this camera convention

The starting numbers are illustrative, not ZED calibration. The depth must align with the colour pixel and use the same camera model. This converts to the camera frame only; robot-base coordinates need a separate calibrated transform.
