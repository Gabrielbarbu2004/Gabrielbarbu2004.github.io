01 / MSc THESISVISION + ROBOTICS

Vision-guided robotic docking

A perception pipeline to bring the MARIO robot toward a docking target using camera observations.

The work

Used YOLO target detection and ZED RGB-D data to estimate a target pose. Plane fitting and marker orientation helped form a geometric estimate that could be transformed from camera coordinates into the robot’s frame.

The test

Connected pose estimates to a staged approach: move, observe again and refine. Tested the pipeline on the physical MARIO robot under changes in range, orientation and lighting.

02 / GROUP DESIGNROBOT DESIGN

On-orbit servicing arm

A satellite-servicing concept with a three-arm configuration and a six-degree-of-freedom manipulator.

The role

Served as project manager for a six-person design group, coordinating the concept and engineering work across the team.

The analysis

Led manipulator design and MATLAB analysis of kinematics, reach and thermal effects. The work was a design study, rather than an operational flight system.

03 / BEng PROJECTEMBEDDED SYSTEMS

Embedded spectrum analyser

An instrumentation project that grew out of a real testing gap in the electronics lab.

The problem

In second year, I could not fully test a circuit with the equipment available. That experience shaped the goal of my third-year project: make frequency-domain measurements more accessible for my circuit and other students’ circuits.

The build

Built an STM32/Arduino-based spectrum analyser around signal acquisition and display. The companion sampling checker shows one of the design decisions behind measuring signals digitally.
